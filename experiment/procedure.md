# Procedure: Drone is Not Responding – II


## Motor Issues

### Check Physical Damage
#### Steps
- Disconnect the drone from the power source by unplugging the **dean plug**.  
- Disconnect the motor from the ESC by detaching the **yellow**, **black**, and **red wires** one by one from the motor arm.  
- Set the **multimeter to continuity mode** by turning the knob to the appropriate setting.  
- Connect the **red probe to the yellow wire** and the **black probe to the black wire** — a beep sound confirms continuity.  
- Connect the **black probe to the red wire** and the **red probe to the black wire** — a beep sound confirms continuity.  
- If all phases produce a beep, the motor has **no physical damage**. If no beep, the motor needs to be **replaced**.  
- Reattach all motor wires to the ESC and turn off the multimeter.  


### Check Connections
#### Steps
- Disconnect the drone from the power source by unplugging the **dean plug**.  
- Click on the frame arm to zoom in and flip it to make connections visible.  
- Check that all **three motor wires (yellow, black, red)** are properly connected to the ESC's socket.  
- If the yellow wire is disconnected, reconnect it to the ESC's socket as shown in the reference video.  
- Similarly check and reconnect wires for all remaining motors.

## ESC Issues

### Check Physical Damage
#### Steps
- Disconnect the drone from the power source by unplugging the **dean plug**.  
- Zoom in on the frame arm to view the **front and back of the ESC**.  
- Reconnect the battery and listen for a **beep sound** — a beep confirms no physical damage in the ESC.  
- If no beep is heard, manually inspect the ESC for visible damage.  
- Check the **capacitor** inside the ESC — if it is burnt or damaged, **replace the ESC** with a new one as shown in the reference video.  
- Similarly check all remaining ESCs.  


### Check Connections
#### Steps
- Disconnect the drone from the power source by unplugging the **dean plug**.  
- Remove the **GPS**, **battery**, and **upper plate** to access the ESC wiring.  
- Zoom in on the ESC connection area and verify that all **ESC wires are properly connected** to the center plate with no looseness.  
- If any wire is loose or disconnected, **resolder** it back to the center plate as shown in the reference video.  
- Similarly check and resolder connections for all remaining ESCs.
